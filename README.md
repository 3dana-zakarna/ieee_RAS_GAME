# ✦ FLAPPYVISION — MULTIVERSE ARCADE 🌌

An interactive Xtreme Bot experience in the university[cite: 1, 3] where players fly through shifting dimensions, collect golden coins, unlock special gift rewards, and control gameplay using real-time AI hand tracking or keyboard inputs[cite: 2, 3]. Built with passion for **IEEE RAS - AAUP**[cite: 1, 3].

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Interactive Xtreme Bot Experience:** A fun, immersive interactive game experience designed for university events[cite: 1, 3].
* **AI Hand Tracking (MediaPipe):** Play hands-free by pinching your thumb and index finger together to make your character flap[cite: 2, 3].
* **5 Multiverse Worlds:** The environment and obstacle themes dynamically shift every 10 points across 5 unique worlds[cite: 2]:
  1. *Galaxy World 🌌* (0+ pts)[cite: 2]
  2. *Crystal World 💎* (10+ pts)[cite: 2]
  3. *Candy World 🍬* (20+ pts)[cite: 2]
  4. *Cyber City 🌆* (30+ pts)[cite: 2]
  5. *Dream Kingdom 👑* (40+ pts)[cite: 2]
* **Character Selection:** Choose from 8 unique custom-designed avatars and vehicles (Classic Yellow, Neon Green, Crimson Phoenix, Cosmic Blue, Cyber Pink, Turbo Rocket, Neon Butterfly, and Classic Flappy)[cite: 2, 3].
* **Coin Collection & Gift Tiers:** Grab golden coins for extra score and unlock tier-based gift rewards as you hit milestones[cite: 2, 3].
* **Immersive Audio & Neon Design:** Built-in Web Audio API sound effects paired with an eye-catching cyberpunk/space neon UI[cite: 2, 5].

---

## 📁 Project Structure

* **`index.html` (Landing Page):** The Arabic RTL welcoming interface featuring cosmic cover art (`cover.png`) and a neon loading screen sequence[cite: 1, 4].
* **`game.html` (Interactive Game Screen):** The main game screen, HUD, bird selection grid, overlays, and MediaPipe hand-tracking panel[cite: 2, 3].
* **`game.js`:** Core game logic, physics engine, collision detection, particle effects, Web Audio sound synthesis, and MediaPipe hand gesture integration[cite: 2].
* **`style.css`:** Futuristic neon styling, keyframe animations, responsive design system, and custom UI components[cite: 5].

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Open the live deployment or host locally[cite: 4].
2. Click **PLAY** on the landing page to load the multiverse[cite: 4].
3. Select your preferred bird/avatar[cite: 3].
4. **Controls:**
   * **Hand Tracking:** Allow camera permissions and pinch your thumb and index finger together[cite: 2, 3].
   * **Keyboard Backup:** Press the `SPACE` bar if you prefer traditional controls[cite: 2, 3].
5. Avoid obstacles, collect gold coins, and enjoy the university's Xtreme Bot challenge[cite: 2, 3]!
